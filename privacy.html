Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ledgraco ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website at ledgraco.com or engage with our services.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you complete our contact form or book a call
  • Information you share in communications with us, including email correspondence
  • During client engagements, financial and accounting data necessary to perform the agreed services

We may also collect basic technical information through standard web server logs, including IP address and browser type, for security and site performance purposes.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and service requests
  • To provide the accounting and professional services described in our engagement agreements
  • To communicate with you about your engagement or our services
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Client data and confidentiality

All client financial data and engagement information is handled under a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement. Client data is stored in a Microsoft 365 environment with access controls and multi-factor authentication. We do not retain client data beyond the engagement period unless required by applicable law or agreed in writing.

4. International data transfers

Ledgraco is based in India. If you are located in the European Union, United Kingdom, Australia, or Canada, your information may be transferred to and processed in India. We take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled securely and in accordance with applicable privacy laws. If you have questions about cross-border data transfers, please contact us.

5. Cookies

Our website may use basic session cookies for site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use this website.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at dinesh@ledgraco.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Data retention

We retain contact and enquiry information for a reasonable period to manage our business relationship with you. Client engagement data is retained as required by applicable professional and legal standards, and then securely deleted.

8. Security

We maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. These include Microsoft 365 security controls, multi-factor authentication, access-controlled file storage, and password management. No system is completely secure; if you have concerns about a specific data security matter, please contact us directly.
