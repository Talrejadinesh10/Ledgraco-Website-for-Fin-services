About Ledgraco

Built on experience, not ambition

Ledgraco is a CA-led professional services firm delivering offshore accounting and audit support. We are not a platform, a marketplace, or a staffing agency. We are a specialist practice led by a Chartered Accountant who has done this work.

DT

Dinesh Talreja

Chartered Accountant (ICAI) · Founder & Engagement Lead · Ledgraco

I qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 2018. Since then, I have spent six years working on US audit engagements — supporting CPA firms and international audit teams with workpapers, reconciliations, financial statement preparation, and audit support across multiple industries and jurisdictions.

Before that, and running in parallel, I spent over a decade managing Indian accounting and tax compliance — GST, TDS, income tax, ROC filings, payroll — for businesses ranging from startups to mid-size enterprises. I understand the Indian regulatory environment from the inside, not from a textbook.

I also spent two years implementing and supporting Workday Financial Management for enterprise clients — configuring finance modules, building reports, and troubleshooting reconciliations for organisations that had just completed a Workday go-live.

I launched Ledgraco because I saw the same problem repeatedly: CPA firms and international businesses were trying to find someone who could handle the technical work — not manage it at arm's length, but actually do it — and who understood both sides of the equation. Not many people do.

What makes Ledgraco different

Named accountability

You know exactly who is doing the work. Dinesh Talreja is the engagement lead on every client file — not a junior resource managed remotely. That is a deliberate choice, not a constraint.

Audit-first mindset

Six years in US audit means deliverables are prepared the way an auditor thinks about them — documented, traceable, and ready for review. Not bookkeeping that needs to be reworked before it can be audited.

No sales layer

When you book a discovery call, you speak with the founder. When you have a question during an engagement, you reach the person doing the work. There is no account manager between you and the work.

Defined scope

We do not try to do everything. We focus on what we do well — bookkeeping, audit support, and month-end close — and we are honest about what falls outside our scope.

Secure by design

Microsoft 365 environment. MFA enforced. NDA signed before any work begins. Access-controlled file storage. Password manager in use. These are baseline requirements, not differentiators — but we take them seriously.

Honest about fit

Not every firm needs offshore support. Not every offshore relationship works. We will tell you on the discovery call if we do not think we are the right fit — and if we are, we will say exactly why and how.
